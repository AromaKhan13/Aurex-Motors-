## Brand & Style
The design system expresses modern bespoke automotive luxury: deliberate, high-contrast, and quiet in its authority. It merges the physical feel of black lacquer, brushed champagnes, and precision-machined aluminum with contemporary digital ergonomics. 

Targeting discerning automotive collectors, luxury performance buyers, and marque connoisseurs, the emotional response is calm confidence, high exclusivity, and mechanical precision. Visual tension is achieved by pairing the heritage editorial grace of Playfair Display with the functional discipline of Inter, framed by deep obsidian backdrops and restrained warm gold accents.

## Colors
The color palette relies on deep black architecture with sharp, disciplined gold punctuation.

- **Primary Accent (`#D4AF65`):** Warm burnished gold. Used for key callouts, brand emblems, focused indicators, and primary visual focal points.
- **Secondary Accent (`#E8C77E`):** Radiant gold highlight. Reserved for hover interactions, active states, and micro-illuminations.
- **Neutral Core (`#0B0B0D`):** Primary canvas background. An obsidian field that absorbs ambient light.
- **Neutral Elevated (`#141416`):** Secondary canvas background and paneling.
- **Surface Tier (`#1C1C20`):** Interactive card and modal background fill.
- **Border Subtle (`#303036`):** Structural line art and structural framing.
- **Border Luxury (`rgba(212, 175, 101, 0.3)`): Hairline metallic trim on focused and premium containers.
- **Typography Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast, pure white editorial headings and vital data.
- **Typography Muted (`#A7A7AD`):** Technical specs, captions, and secondary body details.

## Typography
Typographic scale balances the poetic elegance of editorial automotive print with instrument-panel clarity:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys heritage, bespoke craft, and status. Used for vehicle model names, marquee headlines, and editorial quotes. Kept strictly at regular to medium weights to avoid visual heaviness.
- **Body & Instrumentation (Inter):** Supplies precise, neutral legibility for performance metrics (0-60 mph, bhp, torque), technical sheets, and interactive elements.
- **Label Caps:** Formatted in uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.15em`) for categories, trim badges, metadata flags, and step trackers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column architectural fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Desktop (>=1280px):** Generous margins (`margin: 4rem`) provide expansive canvas boundaries reminiscent of premium physical lookbooks. Gaps between grid units sit at `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Margins drop to `2rem` with `1.25rem` gutters. Multi-column showcase cards transition to 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Margins contract to `1.25rem` to prioritize full-bleed vehicle visuals and touch targets. Complex vehicle tables collapse into vertical attribute decks.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Section transitions utilize wide vertical intervals (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish calm pace and dramatic vehicle reveals.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids glowing neon aesthetics, favoring high-precision dark optical layers and fine physical-like boundaries:

- **Low Tier (Base Showroom Surfaces):** Base layer at `#0B0B0D`. Secondary structural panels sit at `#141416` with a crisp 1px border of `#303036`.
- **Card & Floating Glass:** Utilizes dark glassmorphism: `background: rgba(28, 28, 32, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a dual-border treatment: a 1px border of `#303036` reinforced with an inner stroke of `rgba(212, 175, 101, 0.12)`.
- **Active / Focused Elevation:** Raised cards employ a deep, diffused drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 101, 0.3)`.
- **Modal & Inset Overlays:** Backdrops use deep tinting (`rgba(11, 11, 13, 0.85)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses subtle softening on corner vertices (`0.25rem` base, up to `0.5rem` on prominent cards). This maintains the architectural sharpness of vehicle chassis lines, aerodynamic angles, and precision-cut metal without appearing sharp or brutalist. Circles and pill shapes are reserved exclusively for status dots, filter tags, and pagination indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reserve, Inquire):** Solid gold fill (`#D4AF65`), pure black typography (`#0B0B0D`), weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing (`0.05em`). Hover shifts to `#E8C77E` with zero bloom, only pure tonal brightening. Radius `0.25rem`.
- **Secondary Action (Configure, View Specs):** Transparent background, 1px border (`#303036`), white typography (`#FFFFFF`). Hover shifts border to gold (`rgba(212, 175, 101, 0.4)`) and text to `#D4AF65`.
- **Ghost Action:** Text-only in `#A7A7AD`, hover transitions to `#FFFFFF` with an animated 1px gold underline.

### Vehicle & Specification Cards
- Built on `#1C1C20` or translucent smoked glass (`rgba(28, 28, 32, 0.8)`).
- Hairline perimeter border in `#303036`. Hover initiates a transition of the border to `rgba(212, 175, 101, 0.35)` and lifts the vehicle photograph via a subtle micro-scale (`scale-102`).
- Integrated metric matrices (e.g., V8 Bi-Turbo | 620 HP | 3.2s) use `label-caps` in `#A7A7AD` above numerical data in `headline-sm` (`#FFFFFF`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Background: `#141416`, 1px border `#303036`, text in `#A7A7AD`.
- Selected State: Background `rgba(212, 175, 101, 0.1)`, border `#D4AF65`, text `#D4AF65`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#0B0B0D` with an inset field fill of `#141416`.
- 1px border of `#303036`. Height: 48px. Typography in `#FFFFFF`, placeholder in `#5A5A62`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#D4AF65` with a hairline ambient ring: `0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 101, 0.25)`.

### Lists & Spec Tables
- Alternating subtle transparency rows or clean edge-to-edge dividing borders (`1px solid #1C1C20`).
- Left column (spec label) in `label-md` (`#A7A7AD`), right column (value) in `title-md` (`#FFFFFF`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp square frames (checkboxes, `0.125rem` radius) or circles (radios) with 1px border `#303036`.
- Checked state reveals solid gold core (`#D4AF65`) with pitch-black checkmark.
